Access

Two separate things decide what someone sees. Module access (here) decides which areas of the app they can enter: an access role grants use or manage per module, people can hold several roles, and the highest level wins. Record access inside a module is separate: for engagements it's the team membership, for opportunities it's ownership. Manage sees and edits every record in its module. Admins bypass all of it.
